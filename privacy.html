Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026

Pawly does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Pawly stores

Pawly stores the information you enter about your pets: names, species, breed, birthday, photos, weight, microchip and insurance numbers, veterinarian contact details, vaccine records, reminders, care logs, documents you scan, and receipts. All of it is saved in a local database on your iPhone or iPad and is included in your regular device backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own iOS settings.

What Pawly does not do

  • No account or sign-up. There is nothing to register.
  • No servers. Pawly has no backend and never uploads your data.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Pawly contains no third-party code that phones home.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Smart Scan and document reading

When you scan a vaccine certificate, receipt, or other document, the image is processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework and, on devices that support it, Apple's on-device language model. Images and recognized text are stored only in the app's local database. Nothing is sent to Pawly or to any third party.

Camera and photo library

Pawly asks for camera access only to scan documents, and for photo library access only when you choose a pet photo or a document image. Both permissions are optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings at any time.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Pawly Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and provides Pawly only with a receipt that confirms your entitlement. Pawly never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Exports and sharing

When you export a backup, share a PDF, or share a document, the file leaves the app only through the iOS share sheet to the destination you choose. Pawly has no visibility into where you send it.

Children

Pawly is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Data deletion

Deleting a pet removes all of its records. Settings > Your data > Delete everything wipes the local database. Deleting the app from your device removes all app data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Pawly collects no data, changes would only ever describe new on-device features.
